# Cranny — Swedish (svenska, `sv`) terminology sheet

Register: informal **du**, sentence case everywhere — also menu items, buttons and pane names, as Swedish macOS
writes them (“Leta efter uppdateringar…”, “Avsluta Cranny”, “Visa i Finder”). Buttons, menu items and toggles in the
**imperative** (Öppna, Lägg till, Ta bort, Radera, Visa, Pausa), as Apple’s Swedish System Settings does (“Visa i
menyraden”, “Minska rörelse”). Status words standing alone take the neuter participle (“Kopierat”, “Pausat”,
“Klart”) unless they describe a specific common-gender noun (timern är “Pausad”, “Klar”).
Typography: quotes ”…” (U+201D on both sides), inner ’…’, apostrophe ’, ellipsis …, spaced en dash “ – ” in running
text (never the English em dash), no apostrophe in the genitive (“Crannys”). Compounds with foreign names take a
hyphen (“Bluetooth-enhet”, “Force Touch-styrplatta”, “Cranny-panelen”). Units: pt, px, ms, timer units tim/min/sek.
Error lines in Apple’s style: “Det gick inte att …” / “Kunde inte …”. Cranny is “den” (en app) when a pronoun is
needed, adjectives in common gender (“Cranny är öppen”).

## Deliberate splits (same English, different Swedish)

| English | Swedish | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shortcuts | Genvägar (Quick Apps pane, the app) · Kortkommandon (Settings ▸ Keyboard section) | app vs keyboard shortcuts |
| Paused | Pausat (wing word for Live Activities) · Pausad (timer, media) | impersonal flash vs a common-gender noun |
| Done | Klar (buttons, timer) · Klart (Quick Apps wing flash) | Apple’s “Klar” button; impersonal flash |
| Open | Öppna (action) · Öppen (preview-stage state segment) | verb vs state |
| Dismiss | Dölj (hide an activity, reminder, failed run) · Stäng (close the Reset banner) | |
| App | App (result kind) · Appen (Calendar ▸ Open meetings in) | “Öppna möten i: Appen / Webbläsaren” |
| Add app… | Lägg till app… · Lägg till… (≤ 10-character empty-state chip) | |
| %@ finished | %@ är klar (a shortcut) · %@: klart (a Tray flow) | flow names are verbs (“Förminska”) |

## Measured (BandFont replica, every notch unit 0.8–1.25)

Pausat 44 pt, Igång 36 pt, Klart 31 pt, Fel 19 pt, Hej 21 pt fit the 48-pt medium flash slot; Kopierat (54 pt at 1u)
fits the 72-pt wide slot. Calendar band: “4 m”–“30 m” fit 48 pt in compactLabel (“om 30 m” = 50 pt does not, so
“in %@” is “%@”); “−59 m” / “+59 m” = 41 pt in compact. Join capsule “Anslut” = 35 pt in caption, the same as «Войти».
